## PDF cancellation repair

PDF.js requires an Error reason in its message-stream cancellation callback; an undefined reason could close the browser stream before its worker controller recorded the cancellation. Supply an Error in the abort and failure paths, and reject stale reads before opening the stream and before finishing text assembly. Typecheck and build passed after the change. The focused real sandboxed Electron regression `scripts/smoke-pdf-cancel.mjs` passed 3/3 with zero renderer exceptions: repeated rapid page changes, search and navigation, and closing the PDF tab.
